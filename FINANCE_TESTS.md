# Financial QA
- Normal purchase = 70% author / 30% platform.
- Promoted purchase = 50% author / 50% platform.
- Client cannot alter splits or balances.
- Verified webhook required before unlock.
- Duplicate webhook must be idempotent.
- Failed payment must not unlock content.
- Refund/chargeback must be recorded.
- Withdraw disabled below TSh 50,000 and enabled at/above TSh 50,000.
- Financial transitions are audited.
