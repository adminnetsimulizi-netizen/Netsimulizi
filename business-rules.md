# Business Rules

1. Reader accounts are self-service.
2. Author accounts are created by Admin only.
3. Every submitted story requires an ownership declaration.
4. Publication requires Admin approval.
5. Standard eligible story revenue: author 70%, Net Simulizi 30%.
6. Promoted story revenue: author 50%, Net Simulizi 50%.
7. Withdraw is unavailable below TSh 50,000.
8. Financial calculations are server-side.
9. Payment access is granted only after verified payment confirmation.
10. Copyright complaints and administrative decisions are audit logged.
