# Revenue Rules

STANDARD:
author = 70%
platform = 30%

PROMOTED:
author = 50%
platform = 50%

Withdrawal:
minimum available author balance = TSh 50,000.

The final accounting calculation must occur server-side. If payment-provider fees apply, the commercial terms should explicitly define whether the 70/30 or 50/50 split is calculated before or after those fees.
